SYNTHETIC SAP SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE ARTICLE
Document ID: SAP-KB-004
Topic: Expense Reclassification

1. SYMPTOMS
An expense has been posted to an incorrect
cost center or general ledger account.

2. INITIAL CHECKS
Identify the affected accounting document,
posting period, company code, and
incorrect account assignment.

3. VALIDATION
Confirm the intended cost center or GL
account using approved accounting guidance.

4. CORRECTION PROCESS
Follow the organization's authorized
correction or reclassification workflow.
Do not modify posted financial records
without the required approvals.

5. ESCALATION
If correction is blocked or the appropriate
account assignment is unclear, raise a
support ticket with the masked document
reference, error details, and business impact.
