SYNTHETIC SAP SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE ARTICLE
Document ID: SAP-KB-003
Topic: Accounts Receivable Payment Clearing

1. SYMPTOMS
An incoming customer payment remains
unapplied or cannot be matched to an
outstanding customer invoice.

2. INITIAL CHECKS
Verify the customer account, payment
reference, currency, and payment amount.

3. OPEN ITEM REVIEW
Check whether the expected customer invoice
is still open and available for clearing.

4. PAYMENT DIFFERENCES
Review differences between payment amounts
and outstanding invoice balances.
Do not force clearing of unexplained
differences.

5. ESCALATION
If the payment cannot be matched, prepare
an incident with the customer account
reference in masked form, error details,
and relevant payment information.
