SYNTHETIC SAP SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE ARTICLE
Document ID: SAP-KB-001
Topic: Vendor Invoice Posting Issues

1. SYMPTOMS
A user cannot post a vendor invoice.
The system may display a validation error,
duplicate invoice warning, or posting block.

2. INITIAL CHECKS
Record the exact error message.
Confirm the company code, vendor, invoice
date, currency, and invoice reference.
Check whether required fields are missing.

3. PURCHASE ORDER INVOICES
For purchase-order-related invoices,
check the purchase order reference,
quantities, amounts, and available receipt
information where applicable.

4. DUPLICATE INVOICE WARNINGS
Check whether the invoice reference has
already been processed.
Do not attempt to bypass duplicate controls.

5. ESCALATION
If the error persists, raise a support ticket
with the error text, affected process,
relevant document reference, and business impact.
Do not include confidential financial data.
