SYNTHETIC SAP SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE ARTICLE
Document ID: SAP-KB-002
Topic: Tax Determination and Validation

1. SYMPTOMS
An invoice or accounting document cannot be
posted because of a tax-related validation
error or unexpected calculated tax amount.

2. INITIAL CHECKS
Record the exact error message.
Identify the company code, transaction type,
tax jurisdiction, and selected tax code.

3. TAX CODE VALIDATION
Verify that the selected tax code is
appropriate for the transaction according
to approved organizational tax guidance.

4. TAX AMOUNT DIFFERENCES
Compare the entered tax amount with the
system-calculated amount.
Check whether the tax base and transaction
details are consistent.

5. ESCALATION
Do not change tax configuration or override
tax controls without authorization.
Escalate unresolved issues to the designated
SAP Finance or tax support team.
