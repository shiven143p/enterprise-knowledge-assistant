
NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
IT SUPPORT POLICY
Document ID: IT-001
Department: Information Technology
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE
This policy describes how employees obtain
technical assistance.

2. SUPPORT CHANNEL
Employees must raise IT support requests
through the internal helpdesk portal.

3. PASSWORD RESET
Employees can reset passwords through
the self-service identity portal.

4. VPN ISSUES
Employees experiencing VPN problems should
first verify their internet connection
and restart the VPN client.

5. INCIDENT PRIORITY
Critical incidents receive a target initial
response within 1 hour.

Normal incidents receive a target initial
response within 8 business hours.

6. SECURITY INCIDENTS
Suspected phishing or account compromise
must be reported immediately to the
information security team.
