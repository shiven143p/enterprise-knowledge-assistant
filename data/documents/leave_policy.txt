
NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
Document ID: HR-001
Department: Human Resources
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines employee leave entitlements
and the process for requesting leave.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid
annual leave per calendar year.

3. SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave
per calendar year.

4. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
Employees must submit leave requests through
the HR portal at least 5 working days before
planned leave.

5. APPROVAL
The reporting manager reviews and approves
leave requests.

6. EMERGENCY LEAVE
For emergencies, employees should inform
their reporting manager as soon as possible.

7. UNUSED LEAVE
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days
may be carried forward to the next year.
