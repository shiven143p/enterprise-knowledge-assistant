
NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY
Document ID: TRV-001
Department: Travel and Operations
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes procedures
for authorized business travel.

2. TRAVEL APPROVAL
Business travel requires prior approval
from the reporting manager.

3. BOOKING PROCESS
Employees must book approved travel
through the corporate travel portal.

4. AIR TRAVEL
Economy class is the default for
domestic business travel.

5. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION
Employees must use hotels within
the approved corporate rate limits.

6. TRAVEL EXPENSES
Employees must submit travel expenses
according to the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

7. CANCELLATIONS
Employees should notify the travel desk
as soon as travel plans change.
