
NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Document ID: FIN-001
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs reimbursement of
approved business-related expenses.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
Eligible expenses include approved travel,
business meals, accommodation, and
client-related transportation.

3. RECEIPTS
Receipts are required for expenses
exceeding INR 500.

4. SUBMISSION DEADLINE
Expense claims must be submitted within
30 calendar days of the expense date.

5. APPROVAL
Claims require approval from the
employee's reporting manager.

6. REIMBURSEMENT TIMELINE
Approved claims are normally processed
within 10 business days.

7. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
Personal shopping, leisure activities,
and unapproved upgrades are not reimbursable.
